## What the desk tracks

- **SPS (Nuremberg)** — the automation show where the gateway and protocol-converter vendors exhibit; the desk's primary verification trip each year.
- **Protocol and standards milestones** — OPC UA FX conformance work, Sparkplug specification activity, and EU Cyber Resilience Act implementation dates, covered in the [industry section](/industry/) as they land.

Findings from events enter the device database only when they are confirmed in manufacturer documentation; show-floor statements are reported as statements, attributed to the booth that made them.
