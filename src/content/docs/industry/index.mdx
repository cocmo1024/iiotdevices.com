## How the desk works

- **Data first.** Recurring coverage grows out of the device database: when a field is missing across an entire product class, that absence is the story.
- **Shows are verification trips.** Event coverage publishes its checklist before the show and its findings after — SPS 2026 results land on 1 December 2026.
- **No vendor copy.** Vendor claims are attributed as claims; specifications enter coverage only from manufacturer documentation, with the source named.
